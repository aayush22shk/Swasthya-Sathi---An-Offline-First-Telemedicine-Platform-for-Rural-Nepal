Problem Context & Motivation

Why Swasthya Sathi?

The severe healthcare accessibility barriers facing millions in rural and remote Nepal.

🚨
1:1,700

Doctor Shortage

National doctor-to-patient ratio lags behind WHO norms. In mountain districts, the ratio drops past 1:10,000+, leaving entire municipalities with zero specialists.

🏔️
6–12 hrs

Geographical Isolation

Rugged Himalayan terrain requires patients to walk hours on foot over steep trails just to visit a primary health post, incurring heavy travel costs and lost daily wages.

📶
0% Uptime

Digital Disconnect

Rural cellular networks suffer from frequent dropouts. Existing urban telemedicine apps assume constant 4G/5G broadband and crash or lose data when disconnected.

Impact & Purpose

What Swasthya Sathi Solves

Four targeted breakthroughs addressing Nepal's unique geographical, clinical, and linguistic challenges.

⚡

1. Overcomes Intermittent Connectivity

Designed with an offline-first mindset. Local session persistence and data caching ensure patients can browse prescriptions, check doctor schedules, and log vitals even during complete internet blackouts.

🩺

2. Manages Chronic Conditions Early

Combats undetected hypertension and diabetes through continuous vitals logging (Blood Pressure, Glucose, Weight). Automatically computes historical trend graphs to identify rising health risks before emergencies occur.

🧠

3. Bridges the Health Literacy Gap

Translates complex medical numbers into plain, conversational language in both Nepali and English via generative AI so patients clearly understand their readings and follow doctor instructions.

🇳🇵

4. Native Trust & Payment Ecosystem

Bypasses inaccessible international credit cards through native eSewa digital wallet integration, and enforces practitioner trust through mandatory Nepal Medical Council (NMC) license verification.

Intelligent Engineering

Advanced & Intelligent Features

Combining evidence-based clinical rules with state-of-the-art AI and resilient architecture.

🔄

Offline-First Resilience

Local caching of user sessions, appointments, and prescriptions. Built-in network-state detection allows full offline review of medication instructions without cellular signals.

📈

Statistical Trend Engine (Python Microservice)

Powered by pandas & NumPy. Evaluates linear regression slopes across 7, 30, and 90-day vitals history with adaptive noise filtering to classify trajectories as increasing, decreasing, or stable.

⚠️

Deterministic Clinical Rule Engine

Hardcoded medical safety logic aligned with international standards:
• JNC 8 Guidelines: Flags Hypertensive Crisis (Systolic ≥ 180 or Diastolic ≥ 120).
• ADA 2024 Guidelines: Flags Severe Hypoglycemia (Glucose < 54 mg/dL).

🤖

Google Gemini Generative AI Assistant

Delivers culturally aware, bilingual plain-language health explanations. Enforces strict ethical guardrails (zero diagnosis/prescriptions, mandatory disclaimers, and strict PII anonymization).

User Journeys

Workflow of Doctor & Patients

A seamless, connected care loop designed for ease of use across different literacy levels.

👤

Patient Journey

1
Phone Registration: Quick signup with local session persistence.
2
Doctor Discovery: Filter verified specialists by department, fee, and rating.
3
Book & Pay (eSewa): Select available time slot and pay consultation fees.
4
Log Vitals & Chat: Record BP/Glucose and join live consultation chat.
5
Prescription & AI Help: View treatment plan with AI plain-language translation.
👨‍⚕️

Doctor Journey

1
NMC Registration: Submit official Nepal Medical Council credentials.
2
Manage Schedule: Configure working days, slot intervals, and consultation fees.
3
Review Vitals: Inspect longitudinal trend charts and clinical alert flags.
4
Consult & Prescribe: Conduct chat consultation and issue digital prescriptions.
5
Schedule Follow-up: Set automated follow-up dates to monitor recovery.
